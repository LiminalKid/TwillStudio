TWILL STUDIO FOR KNULLI

A piano-roll groovebox. It needs nothing else installed (not PortMaster).

INSTALL
1. Unzip TwillStudio-KNULLI.zip and copy what's inside, the file "Twill Studio.sh" and the folder
   "twill", into roms/ports on the card's SHARE partition (on the handheld that is
   /userdata/roms/ports). If it asks about existing files, choose merge and replace.
2. On the handheld: Start > Game Settings > Update Gamelists.
3. Start Twill Studio from Ports.

QUIT
Select + Start, Menu + Start, or Quit Twill at the end of the main menu. The song is saved.

UPDATE
Unzip the new version over the old one. Your songs stay. (If Twill Studio was installed through
PortMaster, this replaces the program and its launcher and keeps your songs.)

YOUR FILES, in roms/ports/twill
projects/   saved songs (the one you're working on is also saved as autosave.twill)
samples/    your own samples
renders/    songs rendered to WAV, and stems
log.txt     what happened on the last run, for bug reports
